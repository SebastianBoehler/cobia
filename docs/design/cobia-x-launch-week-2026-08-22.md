# Cobia X launch week

Status: active operating plan

Window: 2026-08-22 through 2026-08-28, Europe/Berlin

This is a seven-day post-submission campaign for @Cobia_Web3. Its goal is to
make the verified-intent boundary familiar through current proof and useful
participation. It is not permission to automate social actions.

## Operating rule

**AI proposes. Cobia verifies. You keep the keys.**

Every main post connects to that boundary: the user states an outcome, solvers
propose, Cobia independently checks the exact program against signed limits,
and only the owner wallet approves calls. Never turn planned, paused, estimated,
or UI-only state into a live-execution claim.

## Daily budget

The heartbeat may prepare, but never autonomously take, the following actions:

| Activity | Daily maximum | Standard |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Main-timeline posts | 3 candidates | Publish only a real proof, useful explanation, or timely pulse post. |
| Replies | 1 candidate per window | Be specific to the original conversation before mentioning Cobia. |
| Likes | 2 candidates per window | Like only material Cobia can sincerely endorse. |
| Follows | 3 total in the week | Verify account and relevance; never turn follows into a quota. |
| Reposts/quote posts | 1 candidate per day | Add distinct context; never amplify a claim Cobia has not checked. |

The main-post ceiling is not a quota. Respect a 3-5 hour gap, vary the lane and
format, and stop when there is no current proof or meaningful conversation.

## Heartbeat windows

Run three campaign checks per day at staggered local times: 10:17, 14:17, and
18:17. The current window starts immediately; after that, the next scheduled
check is used. Each check inspects the live timeline, replies/notifications,
relevant ecosystem conversations, current product state, and evidence before
preparing a compact approval slate.

| Window | Primary job | Secondary job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | Scan proof and ecosystem shipping | One useful reply or follow candidate |
| Midday | Strongest main-post candidate | Two selective like candidates |
| Evening | Community pulse or thread continuation | Reply, quote-post, and next-window learning |

Every slate shows the exact copy/action, destination account, links/media,
cashtags/mentions, reason, and repetition check. No post, reply, like, repost,
or follow happens until the user confirms that item.

receipt, cite a prior receipt as prior proof or choose a different lane.

## Distribution rules

- Use `$TICKER` only for a verified asset that is truly the subject of the
  post. A cashtag is discovery, not a trade recommendation.
- Use `@account` only for a relevant chain, protocol, product, or builder. For
  example, mention @solana or @base only when the post actually concerns that
  ecosystem.
- Tag one account and one asset by default. Add another only when the relationship
  is the content of the post.
- Keep an original visual or product capture distinct from any inspiration.
- Do not quote-tweet, like, or reply to loss, exploit, liquidation, or security
  incidents with a joke or promotion.

## End-of-week review

At the final window, record only useful signals: receipt/program-link clicks,
qualified profile visits, direct product questions, reproduction attempts,
relevant builder conversations, and repeat intent starts. Likes and impressions
are context, not a reason to repeat low-value content. Continue only the lanes
that produced informed interaction or verified-product interest.
