# Cobia X launch week

Status: active operating plan

Window: 2026-08-22 through 2026-08-28, Europe/Berlin

This is a seven-day post-submission campaign for @Cobia_Web3. Its goal is to
make the verified-intent boundary familiar through current proof and useful
participation. It is not permission to automate social actions.

Use `cobia-x-crypto-native-voice.md` for replies and community-pulse delivery.
Borrow CT's rhythm, references, and visual-remix instinct; never borrow price
hype, token endorsement, forced slang, raids, or artificial engagement.

## Operating rule

**AI proposes. Cobia verifies. You keep the keys.**

Every main post connects to that boundary: the user states an outcome, solvers
propose, Cobia independently checks the exact program against signed limits,
and only the owner wallet approves calls. Never turn planned, paused, estimated,
or UI-only state into a live-execution claim.

The heartbeat may prepare, but never autonomously take, the following actions:

| Follows | 3 total in the week | Verify account and relevance; never turn follows into a quota. |
| Reposts/quote posts | 1 candidate per day | Add distinct context; never amplify a claim Cobia has not checked. |

The main-post ceiling is not a quota. One strong anchor is the expected daily
rhythm. A second is conditional and requires a materially new result or
audience. Respect a four-hour gap, vary the lane, audience, hook, and format,
and stop when there is no current proof or meaningful conversation.

## Heartbeat windows

Run five campaign checks per day at staggered local times: 10:17, 12:17, 14:17,
15:17, and 18:17. Actual interaction may move by roughly 10-20 minutes when that
makes the timing more natural. Each check inspects the live timeline,
replies/notifications, relevant ecosystem conversations, current product state,
and evidence before preparing a compact approval slate.

| Window | Primary job | Secondary job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | Find the day's strongest outcome and the live conversation it belongs in | Prepare one useful reply or follow candidate; no main post by default |
| Mid-morning | One community or meme reply to a current, directly relevant post | Use original Cobia media or a one-line native response; no CTA by default |
| Midday | Publish one self-contained anchor candidate | Prepare founder quote-post copy and up to two selective likes |
| Afternoon | Founder amplification or a useful reply around the anchor | Skip when it would repeat the brand post or force a product mention |
| Evening | Distribute the anchor through a contextual reply or quote-post | Review questions and evidence for the next anchor; second main post only if it passes the exception rule |

For evening pulse work, prefer one recognisable CT or builder moment, one native
line, and one original or clearly contextual visual. No CTA by default.

For meme replies, use `post-specific setup -> Cobia-native twist -> stop`.
The joke must still make sense without a product link. Prefer wallet review,
solver competition, explicit limits, shipping, or intent-resolution moments.
One meme reply per day is enough; a second community reply should be useful,
supportive, or technically specific.

Every slate shows the exact copy/action, destination account, links/media,
cashtags/mentions, reason, and repetition check. Founder amplification must name
the exact account. No post, reply, like, repost, or follow happens until the
user confirms that item.

## Distribution diagnosis

The Aug 7-23 comparison shows that Cobia already publishes more aggressively
than nearby BuildX accounts. Its constraint is reach and comprehension, not
inventory. Optimize each day for one clear object and its distribution:

1. Open with an outcome: exact value moved, an unsafe program rejected, or a
   solver improvement measured.
2. Make the visual understandable without reading a thread.
3. Put the receipt, program, or inspectable page directly beneath the result.
4. Add founder context through a quote-post 15-90 minutes later.
5. Join one existing X Layer or BuildX conversation where the object is useful.
6. Publish a follow-up only when the result changes; mechanism restatements stay
   in replies or documentation.

Displayed likes and replies are not assumed to be independent demand. Track
qualified profile visits, evidence clicks, product questions, and builder
conversations instead of copying reciprocal engagement patterns.

## Distribution guardrails

- Target one self-contained anchor per day. A second main post requires a fresh
  outcome, measured result, distinct audience, or genuinely timely pulse.
- Every anchor must pass the hook gate: a reader can understand the result from
  the first line and visual without opening a thread.
- Complete or explicitly skip the founder quote-post and contextual ecosystem
  contribution before treating another Cobia main post as the priority.
- Never publish two prompt-entry clips, two wallet-review clips, or two solver
  recruitment clips in adjacent windows.
- Morning and evening windows default to distribution, reply, or no-op—not
  additional brand posts.
- Use the vertical cut once in the week. Do not upload the same edit again merely
  with a different crop.
- Record every used filename and permalink immediately after publication so the
  heartbeat cannot propose the same media twice.

## Distribution rules

- Use `$TICKER` only for a verified asset that is truly the subject of the
  post. A cashtag is discovery, not a trade recommendation.
- Use `@account` only for a relevant chain, protocol, product, or builder. For
  example, mention @solana or @base only when the post actually concerns that
  ecosystem.
- Tag one account and one asset by default. Add another only when the relationship
  is the content of the post.
- Keep an original visual or product capture distinct from any inspiration.
- Do not quote-tweet, like, or reply to loss, exploit, liquidation, or security
  incidents with a joke or promotion.

## End-of-week review

At the final window, record only useful signals: receipt/program-link clicks,
qualified profile visits, direct product questions, reproduction attempts,
relevant builder conversations, and repeat intent starts. Likes and impressions
are context, not a reason to repeat low-value content. Continue only the lanes
that produced informed interaction or verified-product interest.
